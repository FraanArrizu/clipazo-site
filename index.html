Clipazo

Turn stream highlights into short vertical clips.

Clipazo is a desktop tool for streamers. It finds the moments of your own streams that are worth sharing and exports them as 9:16 clips, ready to review and post to TikTok.

How it works

  1. Analyze. Clipazo scans a recording of your stream (or a stream you have permission to use) for loud moments, chat spikes, scene changes and key words.
  2. Export. It cuts the best moments into short vertical clips on your computer.
  3. Review and post. You choose a clip and Clipazo sends it to your TikTok inbox as a draft. You open TikTok, check the video, write the caption and publish it yourself.

What Clipazo does not do

  • It never publishes to TikTok automatically.
  • It does not read your followers, messages or other account data.
  • It is meant for your own streams or content you have permission to use.

Clipazo is an independent project and is not affiliated with TikTok or Kick.
