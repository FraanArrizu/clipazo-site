Clipazo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clipazo runs on your own computer. The developer does not operate a server that receives your videos, clips or account data.

What stays on your computer

  • The stream recordings you analyze and the clips Clipazo creates.
  • The analysis results (timestamps, scores and transcripts of clips).
  • Your TikTok access token, if you connect your account. It is stored locally so you do not have to sign in every time.

What Clipazo reads from other services

  • Kick: public information about a stream or video and its public chat messages, to find highlight moments.
  • TikTok (only if you connect your account): a basic profile identifier and display name, used only to show which account is connected, and permission to upload a video to your inbox as a draft.

What Clipazo does not do

  • It does not sell, share or send your data to the developer or to third parties.
  • It does not read your TikTok followers, messages or other account data.
  • It does not publish to TikTok on its own.
  • It does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

Speech transcription

To find key words, Clipazo transcribes the audio of short moments locally on your computer. The audio is not uploaded anywhere for this.

Your choices

You can disconnect Clipazo from TikTok at any time in your TikTok account settings, and you can delete the stored token and any recordings, clips and results by removing Clipazo's files from your computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
